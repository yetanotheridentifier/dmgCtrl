# X-Wing: Setup Help

dmgCtrl supports the **X-Wing Alliance (XWA)** ruleset.

## Match Type

- **Casual** — a standard pick-up game with no round limit.
- **Tournament** — shows the **Rounds** stepper so you can set the maximum number of rounds (2–10).

## Scenario

Choose the scenario for this game:

- **None** — no scenario; mission points come from ship kills only (casual deathmatch).
- **Assault at the Satellite Array**
- **Chance Engagement**
- **Salvage Mission**
- **Scramble the Transmissions**
- **Ancient Knowledge**

Tap the scenario to choose it from a list, or the **dice button** to pick one at random from the five named options.

The scenario selection is not saved between sessions — choose it fresh each game.

## Choosing Lists

Each player has a card. Tap it to choose that player's list from a sheet of your saved squads, each shown with its points total and faction.

- **Your list** is remembered, so next time it is already chosen. Tap **Change list** to pick another.
- **Your opponent** is chosen each game. Their card asks **Who are you playing?** and offers last game's opponent in one tap; **Choose another list** opens the sheet.
- **Import XWS** in the sheet opens the **Squad Lists** screen, where you paste a squad from YASB and save it. Tap **Back** to return; your choices so far are kept and the new list is ready to pick.
- **No list** comes last, as a fallback: there are no ships to tap on the game screen, so you set the player's mission point deficit (0–4) with the stepper on their card. The deficit is added to the opponent's score if all of that player's ships are destroyed.

A chosen list shows its points, faction and pilots so you can check it before starting. Its deficit is worked out for you as `50 − total points` (0 for a 50-point list) and isn't shown.

**Go to game** becomes available once both players have chosen.

Your choices, the scenario and the deficits are kept while you visit Squad Lists, Help or Settings, and after a game — tap **Back** on the game screen for a rematch with the same lists. Apart from your list, they reset when you leave X-Wing for the game select screen.

To rename or delete saved squads, tap **More** (top right) then **Squad lists**. Help and Settings are there too.

### Saved lists

Squads are created on the Squad Lists screen from an XWS export. A valid list must:

- Contain between 3 and 8 ships.
- Have a total points cost between 46 and 50.
