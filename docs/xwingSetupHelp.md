# X-Wing: Setup Help

dmgCtrl supports the **X-Wing Alliance (XWA)** ruleset.

## Match Type

- **Casual** — a standard pick-up game with no round limit.
- **Tournament** — enables the **Rounds** stepper so you can set the maximum number of rounds (2–10).

## Scenario

Choose the scenario for this game:

- **None** — no scenario; mission points come from ship kills only (casual deathmatch).
- **Assault at the Satellite Array**
- **Chance Engagement**
- **Salvage Mission**
- **Scramble the Transmissions**
- **Ancient Knowledge**

Tap the **dice button** to pick a scenario at random from the five named options.

The scenario selection is not saved between sessions — choose it fresh each game.

## Choosing Lists

Choose **Your list** first; the **Opp list** section appears once you have. Each player chooses independently:

- **No list** — enter the player's mission point deficit (0–4) with the stepper. The deficit is added to the opponent's score if all of that player's ships are destroyed.
- **＋ New list** — opens the **Squad Lists** screen, where you paste a squad from YASB and save it. Tap **Back** to return; your choices so far are kept and the new list is ready to pick.
- **A saved list** — each saved squad is listed with its points total and faction. Tap one to choose it; its pilots are shown so you can check it before starting. The deficit is worked out for you as `50 − total points` (0 for a 50-point list).

Tap the **edit button** next to a choice to pick again — changing one player's choice does not affect the other. Once both players have chosen, the **Go to game** button appears at the bottom-right.

Your choices, the scenario and the deficits are kept while you visit Squad Lists, Help or Settings, and after a game — tap **Back** on the game screen for a rematch with the same lists. They reset when you leave X-Wing for the game select screen.

To rename or delete saved squads, tap the **list button** in the header to open Squad Lists.

### Saved lists

Squads are created on the Squad Lists screen from an XWS export. A valid list must:

- Contain between 3 and 8 ships.
- Have a total points cost between 46 and 50.
