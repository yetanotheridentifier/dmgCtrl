# dmgCtrl: Help

## About

dmgCtrl is a damage tracker for Star Wars Unlimited. For the best experience, install it on your iPhone via Safari, tap the Share button, then **Add to Home Screen**.

## Getting Started

Open the app and the loading screen appears for a moment while base data is fetched from the internet. Once ready, the **Star Wars: Unlimited** setup screen opens.

Setup has four things: **Mode**, **Your base** (and **Their base** with 2 players), **Match**, and the button that starts play.

### Mode

Choose **1 player** to track your base alone, or **2 players** to set up a base for each side. Your choice is kept for next time; the first time it is 1 player.

With 2 players the game screen still tracks your base only for now: **Go to game** opens the same game as 1 player, and their base is kept in setup for next time.

### Your base

The first time, the card reads **Choose your base**. Tap it to open the base sheet.

Once chosen, your base is kept: it is there next game, and next time you open the app. The card shows its art, its aspect, name, set and HP. Tap **Change base** to pick another.

### Their base

With 2 players, a **Their base** card sits beside yours. The first time it reads **Choose their base**, and the start button waits with "Choose their base to start." Tap the card, or **Change base** once there is one, to open the same base sheet as yours. Their base has no Favourite, and it never joins your Recent bases.

Their base is kept from your last 2-player game, and the card says **Same as last game** while it is unchanged.

### Choosing a base

The base sheet lists:

- **Favourites** first, then **Recent**: the last 6 bases you played, without repeating a favourite. The first time, before you have either, every base is listed.
- A **search** box: type part of a base's name. Every base is searched.
- Four **aspect** buttons (Vigilance, Command, Aggression, Cunning): tap one to list only that aspect, with or without a search. Tap it again to clear it.

Each base shows its aspect, name, set and HP, and your current base has a tick. Tap a base to choose it; the sheet closes. Close the sheet with × to keep the base you had.

### From an SWUDB deck

If you have an unlisted or public deck on swudb.com, its base comes with the link:

1. In the base sheet, tap **Paste SWUDB link**
2. Paste your deck link (e.g. `https://swudb.com/deck/AbCdEfGhI`). Edit links are corrected to view links
3. Tap **Load**: the deck name appears with its base
4. Tap **Use this base**, or the base itself

A link that isn't a deck says **Invalid deck URL**; a private deck says **Deck not accessible**; a deck whose base the app doesn't know says **Base not recognised**. Tap **Search instead** to go back.

### Favourites

Tap **Favourite** on the Your base card to save your base as a favourite: the star fills. Tap it again to remove it. Favourites come first in the base sheet.

### Match

Choose **Casual** or **Tournament**; your choice is kept. With Casual, **Go to game** opens the game. With Tournament, **Start tournament** opens a sheet where you set the format, best of 1 or 3 and the rounds, then **Start round 1** goes straight to the game. While a tournament is under way the button reads **Go to tournament** and takes you back to it.

The button is disabled until you have chosen a base, and with 2 players until you have chosen theirs too. In a tournament, the base sheet lists only the bases legal in its format, for both of you.

### More

Tap ⋯ at the top right for **Help** and **Settings**. Tap <svg width="0.8em" height="0.8em" viewBox="0 0 24 24" fill="currentColor" aria-hidden="true" style="vertical-align:middle;display:inline-block"><path d="M15.5 5L7 12L15.5 19Z"/></svg> at the top left to go back to game select.

Your preferences are saved automatically and persist between sessions.

| Setting | Default | Description |
|---|---|---|
| **Use Hyperspace Art** | On | When enabled, the Hyperspace variant is used by default on the game screen for any base that has one. If the Hyperspace image can't be loaded, the app falls back to the standard art. |
| **Force Token Display** | LOF bases only | Controls when the Force token button is shown: **Always on** shows it for every base; **LOF bases only** shows it only for Force-relevant bases in the Legends of the Force set; **Always off** hides it entirely. |
| **Enable Epic Actions** | On | Shows the Epic Action button on the game screen. Turn off if you prefer not to track epic actions. |
| **Enable Screen Wake Lock** | On | Keeps the screen on during play. May affect battery life. |
| **Enable Action Log** | On | Shows a scrollable log of game actions with an Undo button for the most recent action. Also enables the round tracker button. |
| **Enable Favourites** | On | Lists your saved favourites in Settings. The Favourite star on setup works whatever this is set to. |
| **Enable Long Press** | On | Allows long-pressing or dragging the +/− buttons to apply larger damage or healing values quickly. Turn off if accidental large hits are a problem. |
| **Enable Competitive Mode** | Off | Shows the Bo1 and Bo3 timer settings. Setup always offers Casual or Tournament. See [Competitive Play](#competitive-play) below. |
| **Bo1 Timer** | 25 min | Match timer duration for Best of 1 games. Adjustable in 5-minute steps (5–90 min). Shown only when Enable Competitive Mode is on. |
| **Bo3 Timer** | 55 min | Match timer duration for Best of 3 games. Adjustable in 5-minute steps (5–90 min). Shown only when Enable Competitive Mode is on. |

### Managing saved favourites

When **Enable Favourites** is on, your saved bases are listed below the toggle in the Settings screen.

- Tap **Remove** next to a base to delete it from your favourites
- Tap **Clear All** to remove all saved bases at once — tap **Confirm** to proceed, or **Cancel** to go back

If no bases have been saved yet, "No favourites saved" is shown in this section.

## Format

A casual game has no format: the base sheet lists every base, whatever its set.

A tournament has one: **Premier**, **Eternal** or **Limited**, chosen on the Start tournament sheet and remembered for next time. While Tournament is chosen, the base sheet lists only the bases legal in that format, and an SWUDB deck whose base isn't legal says so.

## Competitive Play

Choose **Tournament** under Match on the setup screen, tap **Start tournament**, and set the format, best of 1 or best of 3 and the number of rounds on its sheet.

| Option | Description |
|---|---|
| **Casual** | No score tracking. Standard play with no timer or result recording. |
| **Best of 1** | Single-game match. Records a win, loss, or draw. Includes a match timer (default 25 min). |
| **Best of 3** | First to 2 game wins. Records each game result and tracks the overall match score. Includes a match timer (default 55 min). |

In a tournament game, the right margin of the game screen shows the match timer and the games score so far. See the game help for how results, draws and the timer work.

## Troubleshooting

**Base art isn't loading:**
Check your connection and try restarting the app. The app needs an internet connection on first load to fetch card data. Once loaded, data is cached on your device for 24 hours.

**The wrong base is showing:**
Tap <svg width="0.8em" height="0.8em" viewBox="0 0 24 24" fill="currentColor" aria-hidden="true" style="vertical-align:middle;display:inline-block"><path d="M15.5 5L7 12L15.5 19Z"/></svg> on the game screen to return to the setup screen and make a new selection.

**Card art isn't loading:**
This can happen if the image server is temporarily unavailable. The app will show the base name and game text in place of the card art, all game functions still work normally.

**Newly previewed bases not selectable:**
Card data refreshes automatically every 24 hours. If a new base from spoiler season is not appearing, try again once published to https://starwarsunlimited.com/ and wait for the cache to refresh.
